Summary

Cindr is an iPhone email-cleanup application. It communicates directly from your device to Gmail. Cindr does not operate a server that receives or stores your mailbox content.

The short version: Cindr does not sell your information, use mailbox data for advertising, upload messages for remote AI processing, or use email content to train machine-learning models.
⌁

Direct connection

Your iPhone talks directly to Gmail when you ask Cindr to load or act on email.

◌

Local progress

Your cleanup progress and preferences stay protected on your device.

↗

Clear actions

Cindr only moves or unsubscribes from messages after your explicit choice.

Information Cindr processes

Account authorization data

When you connect an email provider, Cindr receives OAuth access and refresh tokens, token expiration information, granted scopes, and basic account identity such as an email address or provider account identifier. Cindr never asks for or receives your email password.

Mailbox data

To provide cleanup, Cindr processes message identifiers, sender information, subject lines, dates, short previews, label state, and unsubscribe metadata. Full message content is requested only when you open a message. Cindr does not persist message bodies or attachments.

Your decisions and app state

Cindr stores the local state needed to complete and undo actions, continue an unfinished session, show progress and streaks, remember preferences, and make on-device recommendations. It uses protected one-way sender or domain pattern keys for those local recommendations. Widget data contains aggregate counts only.

Purchases

Apple processes Cindr Plus purchases. Cindr receives subscription status and transaction identifiers from StoreKit only to unlock features. Cindr does not receive payment-card details.

How information is used

  • Display a limited stack of messages for you to review.
  • Open a message you explicitly choose to inspect.
  • Carry out your explicit Keep, Trash, Undo, or unsubscribe decision.
  • Reconnect after token expiration and maintain your Gmail session.
  • Save unfinished cleanup progress and show aggregate history or widgets.
  • Generate transparent, on-device recommendations from aggregate patterns.
  • Protect the app, prevent duplicate actions, and recover safe queued operations after connectivity returns.

Cindr does not automatically permanently delete email. Trash actions move a message to the email provider’s Trash or deleted-items location, subject to that provider’s retention policy.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain using device-only protection. Other Cindr state is stored in protected Application Support files on your device. Aggregate widget values are stored in the Cindr App Group. The app does not persist rendered message bodies or attachments.

Disconnecting Gmail removes its authorization tokens but keeps your local Cindr progress and history on this device, so it can be available if you reconnect. Choose Delete all Cindr data or remove the app to erase that local Cindr state. Provider-side mail remains subject to Gmail’s own retention rules. Apple separately manages App Store purchase history and subscriptions.

Disclosure and sharing

Cindr sends requests directly to Gmail to retrieve data and perform the actions you request. A confirmed standards-based one-click unsubscribe may contact the HTTPS endpoint identified by the sender. If you choose a web or email unsubscribe method, your selected browser or mail app handles it.

Cindr does not sell personal information. It does not share mailbox information with data brokers, advertisers, analytics networks, or AI training services. The production app does not include a mailbox-content analytics service.

Google API Services User Data

Cindr’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing email-cleanup features you request. It is not used for targeted advertising, advertising profiles, creditworthiness, lending, or sale. Humans do not read Google user data except when you provide specific data for support, when required for security or legal compliance, or with your affirmative agreement for a particular purpose.

Cindr requests Gmail’s gmail.modify authorization scope to list messages and perform your explicit Trash or Undo decisions. Read-only access cannot perform those actions, and Cindr does not request Gmail send permissions.

Your choices and controls

  • Disconnect: Remove Gmail from Cindr Settings. Your local progress stays on this device unless you delete it.
  • Delete: Choose Delete all Cindr data to remove Keychain tokens, local and App Group state, and return to onboarding.
  • Google revocation: Revoke Cindr independently through your Google account security settings.
  • Notifications: Control Cindr notifications in iOS Settings. Notifications do not include message sender or subject by default.
  • Subscriptions: Manage subscriptions separately through your Apple account.

See the Data Deletion page for detailed instructions. To ask a privacy question or request assistance, contact dukesbarlow.web@gmail.com.

Security

Cindr uses OAuth with PKCE, the system authentication session, iOS Keychain, file Data Protection, encrypted HTTPS connections, state validation, and narrowly scoped provider permissions. Message HTML is reduced to inert text; remote images, tracking pixels, and JavaScript are not loaded in the native viewer.

No security system is perfect. If you believe you found a vulnerability, follow the instructions on the Security page.

Children

Cindr is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required to independently use the connected email provider in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect information from children through a Cindr-operated service.

International processing

Your email provider and Apple may process information in the locations described by their own policies. Cindr’s mailbox processing occurs on your device and through direct connections to the provider you select.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product or legal requirements change. The new effective date will appear above. Material changes will be communicated through the website, the app, or another appropriate channel.
